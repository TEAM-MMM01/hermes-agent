## 1. Scope of services

Consultant will perform an AI Diagnostic for Client consisting of:

1. Review of Client's completed intake form.
2. One structured diagnostic session of up to [DURATION] by video or phone.
3. A written AI Prescription (Section 2).
4. One delivery call of up to [DURATION] to walk through the Prescription.

## 2. Deliverables

The **AI Prescription** is a written report that identifies up to five business bottlenecks and, for each recommended item: the outcome to achieve, its priority, a conservative expected-impact range, a confidence level, the risks and approvals it depends on, and which delivery option fits (Consultant's Build, Retainer, or Office Hours services, or Client's own team).

The Prescription states outcomes and priorities. It does **not** include step-by-step implementation instructions, configurations, prompts, scripts, or integration work.

## 4. Client responsibilities

Client will:

- Complete the intake form accurately before the diagnostic session.
- Make the decision-maker and relevant staff available for scheduled sessions.

- Identify any regulators, carriers, FMOs, or other parties whose rules apply to Client's marketing, customer communications, or data.

## 5. Exclusions

Unless added through change control (Section 6), this Agreement does not include:

- Implementation, setup, configuration, integration, or automation of any tool or workflow.

- Process mapping beyond what is needed to write the Prescription.
- Data cleanup, migration, or analysis of Client's customer records.
- Staff training.
- Legal, regulatory, compliance, tax, HR, medical, or insurance advice, or any determination that a workflow is compliant.
- Additional sessions beyond those listed in Section 1.
