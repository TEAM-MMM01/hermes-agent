## Clause

1. **Opt-in only.** Consultant will not use Client's name, logo, quotes, results, or story in any testimonial, case study, website, social post, proposal, or presentation unless Client opts in below. Saying no does not affect Client's services or pricing.
2. **Approval of each use.** Consultant will send Client the exact text (and any images) before publication. Nothing is published until Client approves that version in writing (email is sufficient).
3. **No client data.** No Client data, customer data, screenshots of Client systems, or figures from Client's business will be used without Client's separate written approval of those specific items. Restricted data (PHI, Medicare Beneficiary Identifiers, SSNs, and similar) will never be used under any circumstances.
4. **Revocable at any time.** Client may withdraw consent at any time by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL]. Consultant will remove the material from channels it controls within [NUMBER] business days and stop new uses. Consultant cannot recall copies already distributed (for example, printed materials or third-party reposts) but will not reuse them.
5. **Accuracy.** Testimonials reflect Client's honest opinion. Results are described as that client's experience, not a promise of results for others.
6. **Regulated clients.** If Client's industry rules (for example, carrier or CMS rules for Medicare businesses) restrict endorsements or public statements, Client's compliance approver must approve before publication.
7. **Compensation disclosure.** If Client receives anything of value in exchange (for example, a discount), that will be disclosed with the testimonial where required.
